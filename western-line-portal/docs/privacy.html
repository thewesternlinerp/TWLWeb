Portal privacy

The Western Line uses Discord to sign you in and check your membership in the roleplay community.

What the portal uses

When you sign in, Discord shares your user ID, profile name and avatar. The portal bot reads your membership and current server roles. A signed browser cookie keeps you signed in for up to eight hours and is cleared from your browser when you sign out.

Applications, support and role updates

If you send a department application or support ticket, your Discord user ID and submission go to the private review channel. You can view recent submissions and their status in the portal. Western Line leadership can review and update them. Discord can record role changes in the server audit log. Keep personal contact details out of submissions.

Contact

Contact the leadership team through Discord for questions about your data or a submitted application.
